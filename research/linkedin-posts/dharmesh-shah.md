### Post 1

Had one of my most high-stakes speaking engagements of the year today. I was up until 4am last night working on the presentation. The audience? 9,000+ HubSpotters all over the world.One of the slides I shared talks about the 3 chapters of HubSpot.Chapter 1: Democratizing inbound (helping SMBs grow better with inbound marketing). Chapter 2: Democratizing CRM (releasing a legit, free CRM so every SMB could have a CRM). Chapter 3: Democratizing AI (helping millions of SMBs transform their GTM with AI).We're still writing Chapter 3, but it's the most exciting chapter in our history.There are lots of companies looking to help enterprises adopt AI. There are not many that are committed to doing that for SMB. There are almost none that have been focused on SMBs for 20 years.Solving for SMBs has a whole set of constraints that make it a difficult endeavor. You can't deploy an army of FDEs. You can't have your average customer paying you more than $100K+/year. You can't expect customers to just take the leap of faith on AI.I love constraints like this. They breed creativity and reduce competition. Your company is defined by the constraints you self-impose and that you stay committed to.

### Post 3

HubSpot just launched its next generation CLI. We call it the HubSpot Agent CLI because it's built with agentic use in mind.The future of software isn't humans or agents. It's humans and agents. Working together. Humans bring context, judgment, taste, creativity. Agents bring speed, scale, patience, and a shocking willingness to read documentation.Very technical humans have been using command-line interfaces for decades. Why? Because for many jobs, the CLI is simply the fastest, cleanest, most efficient way to get things done inside a system. No hunting through menus. No clicking through seventeen screens.In the modern era, the systems that win will need something else too: AX. Agentic experience. How good is your system at being used by agents? Can agents understand it? Navigate it? Act inside it safely and efficiently?Because the next generation of software won't just be designed for humans clicking around. It will be designed for humans and agents working together. UX + AX. Because AI needs its own UI. That's the new bar.
