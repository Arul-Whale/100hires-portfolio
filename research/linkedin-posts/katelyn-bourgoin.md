### Post 2

The personal brand gurus don't want you to know this but your success in building an audience that actually grows your business is directly correlated to how much proof you share.Not all proof is created equal. There are 8 different types of proof:Tier 1: Borrowed proof. Industry stats or success stories that validate your recommended approach.Tier 2: Self-reported results. Sharing your own wins or numbers. Valuable, but you're the least objective source.Tier 3: Paid media mentions. Being featured in recognizable media outlets or a popular newsletter.Tier 4: Solicited testimonials. A real person vouched for you and sings your praises.Tier 5: Earned third-party recognition. Getting earned media coverage, winning an award, or making a best of list.Tier 6: Unsolicited customer praise. A client publicly gushes about how great you are without being asked.Tier 7: Demonstrated results. A live demo or visible public outcome. You're not claiming results, you're showing them.Tier 8: Endorsement from a trusted source. Someone your buyer already trusts vouches for you.Most experts mostly stick to Tiers 1 and 2. The higher tiers are where clients actually convert.
