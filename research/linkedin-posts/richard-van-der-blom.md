### Post 1

My gift to you, 6 ingredients of high performing LinkedIn Post that boosted my conversion 3.5x over the last 3 months.During the research of millions of LinkedIn Post we found that the best performing ones used more or less the same structure (not AI generated). Implementing these elements will engage your human audience in a better way.Based on 7 years of ongoing research, analyzing over 6M posts in total and writing over 1,000 pages of LinkedIn Insights and Tactics. That's me.1. Hook↳ Probably the most important part of the post. Make people click "more"↳ Use a personal experience as it outperforms any other type of hook↳ Not possible? Go for a negative hook over a positive one2. Promise↳ Don't think when people click "more" you have earned their time↳ They just gave you the benefit of the doubt and 3 seconds more↳ Tell me in 2 lines why should I continue reading, what value do I get3. Positioning↳ New algorithm, 50% of your viewers come from outside your network now↳ This might be the first post they see from you↳ Position yourself, why are you the expert to share this?4. Core Message↳ This is the actual value you wanted to share, the part people actually should read↳ Use formatting, avoid long sentences or paragraphs↳ Don't bore, but inspire5. Conclusion↳ Want conversion? Never, ever, ever let people draw the conclusion themselves↳ What should people get from this, make this message clear6. Call to Engage (CTE)↳ Not be be confused with a commercial Call to Action (CTA)↳ Closed Questions lead to 2,7x more engagement↳ Open Questions lead to 3,4x more conversations in DM's

### Post 2

LinkedIn just launched its Creator Marketplace. Great news, if you live in the US. The rest of us? We wait. Again!This is a pattern of a platform that preaches equality and opportunities for all. The Video Tab. Creator monetisation features. New tools that roll out in America first, while creators in Europe, Asia, Latin America, and the Middle East get no timeline and no explanation.I'm not against LinkedIn building a Creator Marketplace. Connecting brands with creators in a structured way makes sense, and they should have done this way earlier. What I'm against is the assumption that LinkedIn's creator community is American.It isn't.Some of the most consistent, high-quality content on this platform comes from outside the US. And yet, when new features drop, international creators are routinely last in line.Our data suggests LinkedIn has been nudging creators toward official brand collaboration channels for a while now. Toggle the Brand Collaboration label? −45% reach. Add a brand partnership hashtag? −35% reach. Use both together? Up to −65% less performance.That's done by design.LinkedIn's algorithm treats signals of external brand partnerships as a reason to suppress distribution.
