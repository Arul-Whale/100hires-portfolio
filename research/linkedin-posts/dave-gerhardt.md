### Post 2

Spoiler alert: Everything in marketing works.I've done over 300 hours of interviews with CMOs and VPs in B2B marketing. I spent a decade as a marketing leader myself and I can tell you this one thing: Everything in marketing works.YouTube works. LinkedIn works. Direct mail works. Webinars work. TikTok works. Email works. Events work. PR works. Partnerships work.But the challenge is figuring out which channels can work for you with the ingredients you have.You don't always have the product, the features, the timing, the wind at your back, the talent on the team, the budget. But that is the fun part about the job in marketing - you get be the chef, but sometimes there's only two eggs, a slice of bread and an onion left in the fridge. Can you work with that?Too often we try to do everything because we think we have to. And the result is another B2B YouTube channel with 30 videos that all have between 17 and 62 views. You'd be better off picking one channel and doing DEEP there and then finding ways to stack those wins into other channels.Often times GOOD MARKETING is not good for the company. That's right. It's harder to measure, takes time, not obvious, doesn't have UTM parameters in link. And that's what makes it good and makes it work, but you need to have the patience to trust it.
